Shipping & Delivery

Where we deliver, how long it takes, what it costs, and how to track your order.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short

  • We deliver to Waidhan and nearby areas of Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh.
  • Most orders arrive within [[DELIVERY TIME]].
  • Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 or more, and ₹49 below that.
  • Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹25,000.

The fees and limits on this page are today’s. The app’s cart and checkout always show the exact delivery fee and whether cash on delivery is available, before you order.

Where we deliver

We deliver to Waidhan and nearby areas of Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh. Enter your full address and PIN code at checkout. If we cannot deliver to your address, we call you and cancel the order with a full refund.

How long it takes

We pack orders at our shop during shop hours (shown in the app on the Home and Account screens). Most orders arrive within [[DELIVERY TIME]] of being confirmed.

If your order is going to be late, we tell you on WhatsApp or by phone. If it arrives much later than we promised, you can cancel or return it for a full refund.

Delivery fees

Order value (after any discount)Delivery fee
₹999 or moreFree
Below ₹999₹49

There is no extra fee for cash on delivery, and no packing fee.

Cash on delivery

  • Available on orders up to ₹25,000, including delivery. For bigger orders, please pay online with PhonePe.
  • We may switch cash on delivery off for a while. The checkout shows whether you can use it for your order.
  • Pay the delivery person when your order arrives.

Tracking your order

Go to Account → My orders in the app. Each order shows its steps: confirmed, packed, shipped and delivered, and the app sends you a notification at each step. If a courier carries it, the order shows the courier’s name and tracking number. You can also ask us on WhatsApp at any time.

When your order arrives

  • Check that the parcel is sealed and not damaged. If it looks opened, do not accept it, and tell us.
  • Record a short video while you open it. It helps if anything is wrong.
  • Check the IMEI: dial *#06# and match it with the box and your order.
  • Anything wrong? Tell us within 48 hours. See Cancellation & Refunds.

If delivery fails

Please give a phone number we can reach. If we cannot deliver after trying, the parcel comes back to the shop. We then contact you to send it again, or cancel it with a refund.

Picking up at our shop

  • App orders are delivered. Picking up an app order at the shop is not available yet; you are welcome to buy in person at the shop.
  • Repairs happen at our shop. Bring your phone in for the slot you booked. When it is fixed, the app shows Ready for pickup and you collect it from the shop. We do not collect or deliver phones for repair.

Shop: Rajeev Complex, Waidhan, District Singrauli, Madhya Pradesh 486886. Hours are shown in the app.
