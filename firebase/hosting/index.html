About us

Shyam Mobiles is the app of Shyam Online Shop, our shop श्याम मोबाइल शॉप (Shyam Mobile Shop) at Rajeev Complex, Waidhan, in Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh. We are a retailer, wholesaler and distributor of mobile phones and accessories, and we repair phones at our shop.

We sell phones from Samsung, vivo, OPPO, realme, LAVA, Nokia, Xiaomi (Mi) and Apple, along with accessories.

The Shyam Mobiles Android app lets you shop with us from home. Everything you buy in the app comes from our own shop and our own stock.

What the app does

  • Browse and search new phones and accessories, with prices, stock and full specs.
  • Save items to a wishlist and add them to your cart.
  • Pay online with PhonePe (UPI, cards, netbanking) or choose cash on delivery.
  • Follow each order step by step: confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered.
  • Book a repair: pick a date and time slot, approve our price in the app, and follow your repair until it is ready.
  • Ask us anything on WhatsApp from any product, order or repair.

You can browse without an account. To buy or book a repair, sign in with Google, or with a code we send by SMS to your phone.

Why buy from a local shop

Genuine, new products

We sell only new, sealed products. When we pack your phone, its IMEI number appears on your order. Dial *#06# on the phone and check that it matches the box and your order.

Repairs at our shop

We check your phone, send you the price in the app, and start work only after you approve it.

Real people on WhatsApp

Questions about a product, an order or a repair go straight to our shop staff on WhatsApp or by phone.

Refunds you can see

If an order is cancelled after you paid, the order screen shows when your refund is started and when it is done.

Prices and fees

  • All prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include GST.
  • The price on the product page is the price you pay for that item. Your cart and checkout show the full total before you pay.
  • Delivery costs ₹49 on orders below ₹999, and is free on orders of ₹999 or more. See Shipping & Delivery.
  • We charge no platform fee, no cash-on-delivery fee and no packing fee.
  • If you pay by card EMI, any interest or charges come from your bank, and PhonePe shows them before you pay.
  • Repair prices depend on the phone and the problem. We send you the price after we check your phone at the shop.
