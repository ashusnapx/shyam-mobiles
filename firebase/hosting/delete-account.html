Delete your account

How to delete your account in the Shyam Mobiles app (developer: Shyam Online Shop), with or without the app, and what happens to your data.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Shyam Mobiles app and sign in.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm. If you signed in a while ago, the app asks you to sign in again first, to make sure it is you.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Open orders or repairs? You can delete your account only when every order is delivered or cancelled, every repair is completed or cancelled, and any refund we owe you has been paid. If something is still open, the app tells you. Cancel it in the app or wait until it is finished, then try again.

Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

Lost your phone, or removed the app? Contact us from the phone number on your account:

  • WhatsApp +91 79875 59272 with the message “Delete my account”, or call us on +91 79875 59272, or
  • Email shyammobilesshopes7@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and the phone number on your account.

Our staff check that the number is yours: if you write from somewhere else, we call or message that number to confirm. We reply within 48 hours, delete the account within 7 days, and tell you when it is done. If you have an open order or repair, we help you finish or cancel it first.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account: your phone number and your Google sign-in link.
  • Your profile, saved addresses, cart, wishlist, back-in-stock alerts, coupons and referral code.
  • Your notifications and your phone’s notification token.
  • Your repair bookings and the feedback you sent.

What we keep, and for how long

The law makes us keep records of what we sold. After you delete your account, these stay with us but are no longer linked to any account:

RecordWhy and how long
Orders and invoices: items, prices, GST, the name, phone and address the order went to, payment and refund records, and IMEIsGST records. Kept for 8 years (CGST Act, section 36), then deleted.
Security logsYour old account number, the action and the time, with no other personal details. Kept up to 400 days to keep the service safe.
BackupsOur database backups last 14 days, so deleted data is gone from every copy within 14 days.

PhonePe keeps its own records of online payments, under its own privacy policy. Chats you had with us on WhatsApp stay in WhatsApp; ask us and we will delete our copy.

After deletion

You can sign up again with the same phone number or Google account, but your old orders will not come back, because they are no longer linked to you.

Want to delete only some data? You can remove saved addresses and wishlist items yourself in the app, or ask us. See our Privacy Policy for all your rights.
