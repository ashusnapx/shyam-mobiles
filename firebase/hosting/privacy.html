Privacy Policy

What the Shyam Mobiles app collects about you, why, where it is kept, for how long, and how you can see, correct or delete it.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what we need to sell to you, deliver your order and repair your phone.
  • We never sell your data and the app shows no ads.
  • Your card and UPI details go to PhonePe, never to us.
  • Our database is on Google’s servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Usage statistics are collected only if you say yes, and you can say no later.
  • You can download or delete your data in the app: Account → Privacy and Account → Delete account.

Where your data is kept

  • Our database and files (orders, addresses, repairs, invoices) are on Google Cloud (Firebase) servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Google’s sign-in system, phone notifications, usage statistics and crash reports are worldwide Google services, so they may handle data outside India.
  • Online payments are handled by PhonePe in India.

Who handles it for us

Only these companies and people, and only what each one needs:

  • Google (Firebase): sign-in, including the SMS with your sign-in code if you sign in by phone; our database and files; phone notifications; usage statistics if you say yes; crash reports; and a check with Google Play that the app on your phone is genuine.
  • PhonePe: online payments. It receives your order number and amount, and the payment details you type on its pay page, under PhonePe’s privacy policy.
  • Our delivery staff or courier: the name, phone number and address on your order.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): only if you choose to message us there. WhatsApp’s own terms apply to that chat.

We also give data to the government, police or a court when the law requires it, for example to tax authorities. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Your consent, and taking it back

When you first sign in, the app shows a short notice in English and Hindi and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older. Usage statistics have their own tick box, which starts unticked: they stay off unless you tick it.

You can take back your consent as easily as you gave it, at any time:

  • Switch usage statistics, crash reports, back-in-stock alerts or offer notifications off in Account → Privacy.
  • Turn off the app’s location access in your phone’s settings.
  • Delete your account, or ask us to (see Delete your account).

Taking back consent does not undo what we did before. Without an account we cannot take orders or repair bookings from you in the app.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • See your data. Account → Privacy → Download my data gives you a copy of your profile, addresses, orders, repairs, feedback and notifications. You can also ask us for a summary of what we hold and who handled it.
  • Correct it. Edit your name and addresses in the app, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete it. Delete your account in the app or on this website. We keep only what the law makes us keep, as listed above.
  • Take back your consent, as described above.
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
  • Complain. Contact our Grievance Officer first. If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use a right, message or email us from the phone number or Google email on your account. We may ask you to prove it is you before we act. We reply within 48 hours and sort it out within 1 month.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly open accounts for anyone younger. A parent or guardian can sign in and order for a child. If we learn that we hold a child’s data, we delete it.

How we protect it

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels encrypted (HTTPS), and Google encrypts our stored data.
  • Each customer can read only their own account, addresses, orders and repairs. Shop staff see orders and repairs to fulfil them, and staff sign-in needs a second check.
  • Payment keys stay on our servers, never in the app, and every payment is checked with PhonePe.
  • Your sign-in stays encrypted on your phone and is not copied to phone backups.

No system is perfectly safe. If a data breach affects you, we tell you without delay in the app and by SMS or WhatsApp, and we report it to the Data Protection Board of India and to CERT-In, as the law requires.

Messages we send

We send order, payment and repair updates as app notifications. Our staff may call or WhatsApp you about your order or repair. Offer notifications can be switched off in Account → Privacy.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, the app shows you the new notice and asks you again.
