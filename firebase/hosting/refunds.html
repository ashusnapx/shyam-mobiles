Cancellation & Refunds

How to cancel an order, return an item, and get your money back.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short

  • Cancel for free in the app until your order is packed. No cancellation fee, ever.
  • Return unopened items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Damaged or wrong item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and share an unboxing video. We replace it or refund you in full.
  • Online payments go back to the same UPI, card or bank account through PhonePe.

The app shows our current returns policy on every product page and in Account, under Returns & warranty. If it ever says something kinder to you than this page, the app’s wording applies.

Cancelling an order

  1. In the app, go to Account → My orders and tap the order.
  2. Tap Cancel order. You can do this while the order is waiting for payment or confirmed.

Once the order is packed, the Cancel order button goes away. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 79875 59272 as soon as you can. If it has not left the shop, we cancel it for you.

If the order is already on its way, you can refuse it at the door. If you paid online, we refund you once the parcel is back with us.

When we cancel

We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, we cannot deliver to your address, the payment looks like fraud, or the price was a clear mistake. You get a full refund, including any delivery fee, and the order screen shows that it was cancelled.

If your payment comes through late

An online order waits 15 minutes for payment. If your money reaches us after that and the item is no longer in stock, we refund the full amount.

Returns

Changed your mind

You can return an unopened item, with its seal unbroken, within 7 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Bring it to our shop, or send it back at your own cost. The delivery fee, if you paid one, is not refunded when you return something because you changed your mind. Once a phone’s seal is broken we cannot take it back for a change of mind, but the warranty and the rules below still apply.

Damaged, faulty, wrong or fake item

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery by WhatsApp, phone or email, with your order number and photos. An unboxing video helps us sort it out fastest. We replace the item or refund you in full, including the delivery fee, and we collect it from you for free.

We also take back, replace or refund anything that is not as described, or that reached you much later than we promised.

Check the IMEI

Your order shows the IMEI of each phone once it is packed. Dial *#06# on the phone and match it with the box and your order. If it does not match, do not set up the phone: tell us straight away and we will put it right.

What to give back

  • The item with its box, all accessories, manuals and any free gifts.
  • The invoice.
  • If you set up the phone, remove your Google or Apple account and screen lock first, so the phone can be reset.

We check every return when it reaches us. If something is not as described above, we talk to you before deciding.

Warranty after 48 hours

Phones are covered by the brand’s warranty, and we help you claim it at our shop. Bring the phone, box and invoice.

Refunds

You paid byHow you get your money back
UPI, card or netbanking (PhonePe)To the same UPI, card or bank account. We start the refund through PhonePe within 2 working days of the cancellation, or of receiving and checking your return. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show it.
Cash on deliveryIf you cancel or refuse the order before paying, there is nothing to refund. For a return, we refund you by UPI or bank transfer to an account in your name, or in cash at our shop, within 2 working days of receiving and checking the item.

If only some items in an order come back, we refund what you paid for those items. The order screen in the app shows when a refund is started and when it is done. If you have not received it 7 working days after that, check with your bank first, then contact us and we will give you PhonePe’s refund reference.

Repairs

The app does not take payment for repairs, so there is nothing to refund for a repair booking. You can cancel a booking in the app for free until the repair starts. Questions about a repair you paid for at the shop? Contact us.
