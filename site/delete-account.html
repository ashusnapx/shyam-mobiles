Delete your account

How to delete your account in the Shyam Mobiles app (developer: [[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]]), with or without the app, and what happens to your data.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the Shyam Mobiles app and sign in.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Open orders or repairs? You can delete your account only when every order is delivered, cancelled or refunded, and every repair is completed or cancelled. If one is still open, the app tells you. Cancel it in the app or wait until it is finished, then try again.

Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

If you no longer have the app, contact us from the email address or phone number you sign in with:

  • Email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] with the subject “Delete my account”, or
  • WhatsApp +91 79875 59272 with the message “Delete my account”.

Tell us the phone number or email on your account. If you write from a different number or email, we will ask you to confirm with a code sent to the one on your account. We delete the account within 7 days of confirming it is you, and tell you when it is done. If you have an open order or repair, we will help you finish or cancel it first.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account: phone number, email address and Google sign-in link.
  • Your profile: name and phone number.
  • Your saved addresses.
  • Your cart and wishlist.

What we keep, and for how long

The law requires us to keep records of what we sold. After you delete your account, these records stay with us but are no longer linked to any account:

RecordWhy and how long
Orders: items, prices, GST, the name, phone and address the order was sent to, payment and refund records, IMEIs and the order timelineTax and accounting records under GST law. We keep them for 72 months from the due date of the GST annual return for the financial year of the order (about 7 years), or longer if a tax case or dispute about them is open. Then we delete them.
Repair bookings: phone brand and model, the problem, contact phone, our quote and notesService and warranty records. Kept for the same period as orders, then deleted.
BackupsOur hosting provider’s daily backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted data is gone from backups within 7 days.

Razorpay keeps its own records of online payments, under its own privacy policy. Chats you had with us on WhatsApp stay in WhatsApp; ask us and we will delete our copy.

After deletion

You can sign up again with the same phone number or email, but your old orders and repairs will not come back, because they are no longer linked to you.

Want to delete only some data? You can remove saved addresses and wishlist items yourself in the app, or ask us. See our Privacy Policy for all your rights.
