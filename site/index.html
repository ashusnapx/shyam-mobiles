About us

Shyam Mobiles is our app. Our shop is श्याम मोबाइल शॉप (Shyam Mobile Shop) at Rajeev Complex, Waidhan, in Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh. It is run by [[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]]. We are a wholesale and retail seller of mobile phones and accessories, and a sales and repair centre.

We sell phones from Samsung, vivo, OPPO, realme, LAVA, Nokia, Xiaomi (Mi) and Apple, along with accessories, and we repair phones at our shop.

The Shyam Mobiles Android app lets you shop with us from home. Everything you buy in the app comes from our own shop and our own stock. The app is for retail customers.

What the app does

  • Browse and search new phones and accessories, with prices, stock and full specs.
  • Save items to a wishlist and add them to your cart.
  • Pay online with Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets, EMI) or choose cash on delivery.
  • Track each order step by step: confirmed, packed, shipped, delivered.
  • Book a repair: pick a date and time slot, and follow your repair until it is ready.
  • Ask us anything on WhatsApp from any product, order or repair.

You can browse without an account. To buy or book a repair, sign in with a code sent to your phone or email, or with Google.

Why buy from a local shop

Genuine, new products

We sell only new, sealed products. When we pack your phone, its IMEI number appears on your order. Dial *#06# on the phone and check that it matches the box and your order.

Repairs at our shop

We look at your phone, tell you the price, and start work only after you approve it.

Real people on WhatsApp

Questions about a product, an order or a repair go straight to our shop staff on WhatsApp or by phone.

Refunds you can see

If an order is cancelled after you paid, the order screen shows when your refund is issued.

Prices and fees

  • All prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include GST.
  • The price on the product page is the price you pay for that item. Your cart and checkout show the full total before you pay.
  • Delivery costs ₹49 on orders below ₹999. Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 or more. See Shipping & Delivery.
  • We charge no platform fee, no cash-on-delivery fee and no packing fee.
  • If you pay by EMI, any interest or charges come from your bank or card issuer. Razorpay shows them before you pay.
  • Repair prices depend on the phone and the problem. We confirm the final price after we inspect your phone at the shop.
