Cancellation & Refunds

How to cancel an order, return an item, and get your money back.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Cancel for free in the app until your order is packed. No cancellation fees, ever.
  • Return unopened items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
  • Damaged, faulty, wrong or fake item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we replace it or refund you in full.
  • Online payments are refunded to the same payment method. We issue the refund within 2 working days; banks usually take 5–7 working days to show it.

Cancelling an order

  1. In the app, go to Account → My orders and tap the order.
  2. Tap Cancel order. You can do this while the order is awaiting payment or confirmed.

Once the order is packed, the Cancel order button goes away. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 79875 59272 as soon as you can. If it has not shipped yet, we cancel it for you.

If the order has already shipped, you can refuse it at the door. For an online payment, we refund you once the parcel is back with us.

We never charge a cancellation fee.

When we cancel

We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock, we cannot deliver to your address, the payment looks fraudulent, or the price was a clear mistake. You get a full refund, including any delivery fee, and the order screen shows the reason.

If your payment arrives late

An online order waits 15 minutes for payment. If your money reaches us after the order was cancelled and the item is no longer available, we refund the full amount.

Returns

Changed your mind

You can return an unopened item, with its seal intact, within 7 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Once a phone’s seal is broken, we cannot take it back just because you changed your mind, but the warranty and the rules below still apply. Bring it to our shop, or send it back to us at your own cost. The delivery fee you paid, if any, is not refunded for a change-of-mind return.

Damaged, faulty, wrong or fake item

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery by WhatsApp, phone or email, with your order number and photos. An unboxing video helps us sort it out fastest. We will replace the item or refund you in full, including the delivery fee, and we arrange the pickup at no cost to you.

We also take back, replace or refund any item that is not as described, or that we delivered much later than we promised.

Check the IMEI

Your order shows the IMEI of each phone once it is packed. Dial *#06# on the phone and match it with the box and your order. If it does not match, do not set up the phone; tell us straight away and we will fix it.

What to send back

  • The item with its box, all accessories, manuals and any free gifts.
  • The invoice.
  • If you set up the phone, remove your Google or Apple account and screen lock first, so the phone can be reset.

We check every return when it reaches us. If it is not in the condition described above, we contact you before deciding.

Warranty after 48 hours

If a fault appears later, the manufacturer’s warranty covers it under the brand’s terms. Bring the phone, box and invoice to our shop and we help you claim it.

Repairs

The app does not take payment for repairs, so there is nothing to refund for a repair booking. You can cancel a booking in the app for free until work starts. Questions about a repair you paid for at the shop? Contact us.
