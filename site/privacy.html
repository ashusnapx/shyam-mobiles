Privacy Policy

What personal data the Shyam Mobiles app collects, why we need it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can see, correct or delete it.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • We collect only what we need to sell to you, deliver your order and repair your phone.
  • We do not sell your data. The app has no ads, no advertising tools and no analytics tools.
  • The app does not ask for your contacts, SMS or files. It asks for your location only if you tap “Use my current location” while adding an address, and we never store or receive your coordinates.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Account → Delete account.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the companies that help us run the app and deliver your orders, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in system and files.
  • Razorpay processes online payments. It receives your order number and amount, and your phone number and email to fill in its checkout. The payment details you enter in Razorpay’s checkout are handled under Razorpay’s privacy policy.
  • Google signs you in, only if you choose “Continue with Google”. If you use “Use my current location”, your phone’s Android geocoding service (usually provided by Google) may handle your position to find the address; the app does not send it to us.
  • [[SMS PROVIDER]] sends sign-in codes by SMS. It receives your phone number.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes by email. It receives your email address.
  • Courier partners receive the name, phone number and address on your order so they can deliver it.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries your chat with us, only if you choose to message us there. WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that chat.

We also share data when the law requires it, for example with tax authorities, or with police or a court that has a valid legal order.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. Some of our service providers may store or process data outside India.

How we protect it

  • All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Our database lets each customer read only their own account, addresses, orders and repairs. Only shop staff accounts can see orders and repairs to fulfil them.
  • Payment keys stay on our servers, never in the app, and we check Razorpay’s signature on every payment.
  • Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • See your data. Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we shared it with. Most of it is already in the app under Account, Orders and Repairs.
  • Correct or update it. Edit your name and addresses in the app, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete it. Delete your account in the app, or ask us to. We keep only the records the law requires, as explained above.
  • Withdraw your consent. You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it, by deleting your account or by asking us. You can turn off the app’s location access at any time in your phone’s settings. Withdrawing consent does not undo what we did before, and we may no longer be able to sell to you or repair your phone.
  • Nominate someone. Name a person who can use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
  • Complain. Contact our Grievance Officer first. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email [[SUPPORT EMAIL]] or WhatsApp +91 79875 59272 from the email or phone number on your account. We may ask you to confirm it is you before we act. We acknowledge every request within 24 hours and resolve it within 15 days.

Children

The app is for adults aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to sign in and order for you. If we learn that we hold a child’s data without a parent’s consent, we will delete it.

Messages we send

We send sign-in codes by SMS or email, and show order and repair updates in the app. Our staff may also message you on WhatsApp or call you about your order or repair. We do not send marketing messages.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data, we will tell you by SMS, email or WhatsApp before it applies.
