Shipping & Delivery

Where we deliver, how long it takes, what it costs, and how to track your order.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • We deliver to Waidhan and nearby areas of Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh.
  • Most orders arrive within [[DELIVERY TIME]].
  • Delivery is free on orders of ₹999 or more, and ₹49 below that.
  • Cash on delivery is available on orders up to ₹25,000.

Where we deliver

We deliver to Waidhan and nearby areas of Singrauli district, Madhya Pradesh. Enter your full address and PIN code at checkout. If we cannot deliver to your address, we call you and cancel the order with a full refund.

Picking up an app order at our shop is not available yet.

How long it takes

We pack orders at our shop during shop hours (as shown in the app on the Home and Account screens). Most orders arrive within [[DELIVERY TIME]] of being confirmed.

If your order is going to be late, we tell you on WhatsApp or by phone. If it arrives much later than we promised, you can cancel or return it for a full refund.

Delivery fees

Order value (items)Delivery fee
₹999 or moreFree
Below ₹999₹49

The fee is shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay. There are no cash-on-delivery or packing fees.

Cash on delivery

  • Available on orders up to ₹25,000, including delivery. For bigger orders, please pay online.
  • We may switch cash on delivery off for a while. The checkout shows whether it is available for your order.
  • Pay the delivery person when your order arrives.

Tracking your order

Go to Account → My orders in the app. Each order shows its steps: confirmed, packed, shipped and delivered. When we ship it, the order shows the courier’s name and tracking number. You can also ask us on WhatsApp at any time.

When your order arrives

  • Check that the parcel is sealed and not damaged. If it looks opened or tampered with, do not accept it, and tell us.
  • We suggest recording a short video while you unbox it.
  • Check the IMEI: dial *#06# and match it with the box and your order.
  • Anything wrong? Tell us within 48 hours. See Cancellation & Refunds.

If delivery fails

Please give a phone number the courier can reach. If the courier cannot deliver after trying, the parcel comes back to us. We then contact you to send it again or cancel it with a refund.

Repairs

Repairs happen at our shop. Bring your phone in at the slot you booked; we do not collect phones for repair.
